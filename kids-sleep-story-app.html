Frequently asked questions

What is the Cozy Tales kids sleep story app?

Cozy Tales is an Android bedtime story app with illustrated stories and gentle narration, created for family storytime and calming nighttime routines.

Is Cozy Tales free to download?

The app is free to download and includes two free storybooks. Additional storybooks may be available through individual purchases.

Does Cozy Tales require a monthly subscription?

No. Cozy Tales is designed around individually purchased storybooks rather than a recurring monthly subscription.

Does listening to a bedtime story guarantee better sleep?

No app can guarantee sleep outcomes. Cozy Tales is intended as a calming storytime companion, not a medical sleep treatment.
