PetCare Log FAQ

What is PetCare Log?

PetCare Log is a pet care tracker app that helps pet parents organize profiles, feeding logs, medication reminders, vet visits, health history, daily care tasks, and photo memories.

Is PetCare Log only for dogs and cats?

No. PetCare Log is designed for every pet. You can use it for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, and other pets that need daily care, reminders, and health tracking.

Is PetCare Log free?

PetCare Log is free to start. PetCare Log Pro is optional and unlocks unlimited pets, more photo memories, advanced reminders, backup, exports, and premium features.

Does PetCare Log help with medication reminders?

Yes. PetCare Log includes reminder tools for medication, feeding, vet visits, and daily care routines so pet parents can stay organized.
