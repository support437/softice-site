PetCare Log: Pet Care Tracker, Reminders & Health History App

PetCare Log helps pet parents organize daily pet care with pet profiles, feeding tracking, medication reminders, vet visit history, daily checklist tools, pet health records, and photo memories. Whether you care for a dog, cat, bunny, bird, or another beloved pet, PetCare Log keeps everything in one simple place.

PetCare Log FAQ

What is PetCare Log?

PetCare Log is a pet care tracker app that helps pet parents organize profiles, feeding logs, medication reminders, vet visits, health history, daily care tasks, and photo memories.

Is PetCare Log only for dogs and cats?

No. PetCare Log is designed for every pet. You can use it for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, and other pets that need daily care, reminders, health tracking, and photo memories.

Is PetCare Log free?

PetCare Log is free to start. PetCare Log Pro is optional and unlocks unlimited pets, unlimited reminders, unlimited logs, unlimited health records, and premium future features.

Does PetCare Log help with medication reminders?

Yes. PetCare Log includes reminder tools for medication, feeding, vet visits, grooming, and daily care routines so pet parents can stay organized.

Can I track vet visits and health records?

Yes. PetCare Log helps you save vet visits, health notes, medication records, symptoms, allergies, vaccines, and other pet wellness details.

Can I use PetCare Log as a pet photo journal?

Yes. PetCare Log includes photo memories so you can save special moments, milestones, and everyday memories for your pets.
