About Softice Social Intelligence

Softice Social Intelligence is a social-media management, analytics, engagement, moderation, and artificial-intelligence service operated by Softice Innovations Labs LLC.

The service may connect to authorized Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts, and other supported Meta services through official Meta APIs.

Softice Social Intelligence may assist authorized Page owners and administrators with tasks such as:

  • Reading Page posts and engagement activity.
  • Reading public comments associated with authorized Pages.
  • Analyzing engagement and audience interactions.
  • Classifying comments for moderation or response purposes.
  • Generating suggested replies using artificial intelligence.
  • Publishing authorized replies when enabled by the Page administrator.
  • Identifying spam, unsafe, sensitive, or review-required content.
  • Supporting multilingual social-media engagement.

Privacy Policy

Softice Innovations Labs LLC respects privacy and seeks to process only information reasonably necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Softice Social Intelligence.

1. Information We May Process

When Softice Social Intelligence is connected to an authorized Meta asset, information made available through the applicable Meta APIs may be processed.

This information may include:

  • Facebook Page identifiers and Page names.
  • Post identifiers and post content.
  • Public Page comments and comment identifiers.
  • Comment text.
  • Publicly available commenter names or identifiers returned by Meta.
  • Engagement events associated with authorized Pages.
  • Dates and timestamps associated with posts or comments.
  • Page insights or analytics where authorized.
  • Technical event information required to operate Meta webhooks.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Softice Social Intelligence may be used for legitimate service functions including:

  • Displaying or analyzing Page engagement.
  • Detecting and classifying public comments.
  • Identifying spam or potentially unsafe interactions.
  • Generating suggested responses.
  • Providing multilingual response assistance.
  • Supporting authorized automated Page replies.
  • Preventing duplicate automated responses.
  • Maintaining operational and security logs.
  • Monitoring system performance and reliability.

3. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Softice Social Intelligence may use artificial intelligence to analyze social-media content, classify interactions, detect language, generate response suggestions, and determine whether certain interactions should require human review.

Artificial-intelligence processing may be performed using trusted service providers acting as technology processors for Softice.

Sensitive, ambiguous, low-confidence, unsafe, or high-risk interactions may be prevented from receiving an automatic response and may instead require human review.

4. Automated Replies

When authorized by the applicable Page administrator, Softice Social Intelligence may publish automated replies to certain eligible Page comments.

Automated publishing may be restricted by safety controls such as:

  • Minimum confidence thresholds.
  • Language-confidence thresholds.
  • Category restrictions.
  • Spam and safety screening.
  • Human-review requirements.
  • Duplicate-response prevention.
  • Rate limits.
  • Administrator-controlled kill switches.

5. Religious and Sensitive Content

Softice Social Intelligence may classify the content of a message or comment when necessary for moderation and response safety.

The service is designed not to infer or assign a person's religion or other sensitive personal characteristic based solely on comments or interactions.

Sensitive religious discussions, arguments, controversial claims, or similar interactions may be routed for human review rather than answered automatically.

6. Multilingual Processing

Softice Social Intelligence may detect the language of a comment and generate a response in the same language when appropriate.

If the language cannot be identified with sufficient confidence, the interaction may be routed for human review.

7. Storage and Retention

Softice may retain limited operational information necessary to provide and secure the service.

This may include comment identifiers, post identifiers, classifications, response status, timestamps, generated responses, and information required to prevent duplicate replies.

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business, operational, security, legal, or service purposes.

8. Security

Softice uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect service credentials and stored information.

Sensitive credentials such as Meta access tokens, API keys, application secrets, and webhook verification secrets are intended to be stored securely in backend infrastructure and are not intended to be publicly exposed.

Users should never send passwords, access tokens, API keys, authentication codes, or other confidential credentials through public comments or data-deletion requests.

9. Third-Party Platforms

Softice Social Intelligence integrates with third-party platforms, including Meta services.

Information independently controlled by Meta is subject to Meta's own privacy policies, platform terms, and account-management tools.

10. No Sale of Personal Information

Softice Innovations Labs LLC does not operate Softice Social Intelligence for the purpose of selling personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

User Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request deletion of eligible personal information associated with Softice Social Intelligence.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to: support@softiceinnovationslabs.com
  2. Use the subject: Softice Social Intelligence Data Deletion Request
  3. Provide enough information to help identify the relevant record or interaction.
  4. This may include your public Facebook or Instagram display name, the Page involved, and the approximate date of the interaction.
  5. Do not send passwords, access tokens, API keys, login codes, or other confidential credentials.

Verification

Softice may request reasonable verification before processing a deletion request in order to prevent unauthorized deletion of information belonging to another person.

What May Be Deleted

Depending on the request and applicable requirements, eligible information under Softice's control may include:

  • Stored interaction records.
  • Comment or post identifiers retained by Softice.
  • Generated response records.
  • AI classification records.
  • Operational engagement records associated with the request.

Information Controlled by Meta

Some information may be controlled independently by Meta rather than Softice.

Deleting information from Softice systems does not necessarily remove information stored independently by Facebook, Instagram, or another Meta service.

Users may need to use Meta's own privacy, account-management, Apps and Websites, or Business Integrations settings to manage information controlled by Meta.

Retention Exceptions

Some limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, enforcement of agreements, or other legitimate obligations.

A deletion request should never include your Facebook password, Instagram password, Meta access token, Softice API key, or any other secret credential.
