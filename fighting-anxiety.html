Fighting Anxiety FAQ

What is the best way to fight anxiety?

Start with slow breathing, grounding exercises, and small daily habits. CalmPulse combines these techniques into one simple app for everyday anxiety support.

Can breathing exercises reduce anxiety?

Yes. Slow, controlled breathing can help calm the nervous system and reduce physical symptoms of anxiety.

How does CalmPulse help fight anxiety?

CalmPulse provides Panic Reset, guided breathing, grounding prompts, calming sounds, Smart Calm check-ins, and progress tracking to support anxious moments.

Is CalmPulse a replacement for therapy?

No. CalmPulse is a self-care wellness app and does not replace professional medical advice, therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care.
