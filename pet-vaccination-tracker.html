Pet vaccination tracker FAQ

Common questions from pet parents

Can PetCare Log remind me when my pet needs a vaccination?

Yes. You can organize vaccine names, dates, booster schedules and upcoming reminders for each pet.

Can I store my pet's vaccination history?

Yes. You can keep vaccine history and related notes connected to the correct pet profile.

Can I track vaccines for multiple pets?

Yes. PetCare Log keeps vaccine records connected to individual pet profiles. The free version includes one pet profile, while Pro unlocks unlimited pets.

Can I track boosters and yearly vaccines?

Yes. You can record previous vaccine dates and organize the next expected booster or annual due date.

Does PetCare Log replace veterinary advice?

No. PetCare Log is an organization tool and does not replace a licensed veterinarian or professional vaccination advice.
