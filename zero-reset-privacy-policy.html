Zero Reset

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Softice Innovations Labs LLC handles information for Zero Reset – Discipline Streak Tracker.

Effective Date: June 8, 2026

1. Overview

Zero Reset is a discipline streak tracker designed to help users build consistency, track check-ins, monitor streak progress, and receive optional reminders.

We respect your privacy. Zero Reset is designed to store your discipline and streak information locally on your device.

2. Information We Store

Zero Reset may store the following information on your device:

  • Discipline or habit names you create
  • Current streak and best streak data
  • Daily check-in history
  • Failure/reset notes, if you choose to enter them
  • Reminder settings
  • App preferences and Pro purchase status

3. Local Data Storage

Your discipline, streak, check-in, and reminder data is stored locally on your device. Softice Innovations Labs LLC does not operate a cloud account system for Zero Reset and does not collect your streak data on our own servers.

4. Notifications

Zero Reset may request notification permission so it can send discipline reminders. Notifications are optional and can be enabled, disabled, or changed through the app settings or your device settings.

5. Purchases and Billing

Zero Reset may offer an optional one-time Pro upgrade. Purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, receive, or store your credit card number, payment card details, or banking information.

Google may provide limited purchase information needed to verify your Pro access, such as purchase status and transaction entitlement details.

6. Third-Party Services

Zero Reset may use Google Play services for app distribution, billing, crash reporting, security, and platform functionality. These services are governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your discipline streak data with advertisers or third-party marketing companies.

8. Data Deletion

Because Zero Reset stores discipline data locally on your device, you can delete your data by:

  • Deleting disciplines or history inside the app, where available
  • Clearing the app data from your Android device settings
  • Uninstalling the app from your device

9. Children’s Privacy

Zero Reset is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect app functionality and purchase status. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
