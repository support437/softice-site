CalmPulse

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 12, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Softice Innovations Labs LLC handles information in CalmPulse. CalmPulse is designed to provide simple calming tools such as breathing, panic reset guidance, calm timers, ambient sounds, progress tracking, and Smart Calm prebuilt responses.

CalmPulse is designed to be privacy-friendly and does not require an account to use its core tools.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

CalmPulse may store certain information locally on your device to make the app work properly, including:

  • Completed calm session counts
  • Total calm practice minutes
  • Current streak information
  • Breathing, Panic Reset, Calm Timer, and Smart Calm usage counts
  • Selected voice or sound preferences
  • Selected ambient sound preferences
  • Profile display preferences, such as a locally selected profile image
  • Subscription access state as reported by Google Play Billing

3. Smart Calm Responses

CalmPulse uses prebuilt calming responses and categories. CalmPulse does not require you to send personal prompts to an external AI service for the current app experience.

Smart Calm tools are intended to help you choose a calming reset, breathing exercise, or reassurance-style response based on simple in-app options.

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

CalmPulse may offer optional Pro access through Google Play Billing. Purchases and subscription payments are processed by Google Play, not directly by Softice Innovations Labs LLC.

Google may collect and process payment-related information according to Google Play’s own policies. CalmPulse receives subscription status information needed to unlock Pro features.

5. Photos and Profile Image

If you choose a profile image in CalmPulse, the app may save a local reference to that image so it can appear in your Profile screen. CalmPulse does not require you to upload a profile image, and the selected image is intended for local personalization only.

6. Data Sharing

Softice Innovations Labs LLC does not sell your personal information.

CalmPulse may rely on platform services such as Google Play Billing for subscriptions. Those services may process limited information needed to provide billing, restore purchases, and manage subscriptions.

7. Children’s Privacy

CalmPulse is intended for general wellness support and is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Data Removal

Because CalmPulse stores most app activity locally on your device, you can remove local app data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app from your device.

Subscription management, cancellation, and billing history are handled through Google Play.

9. Wellness Disclaimer

CalmPulse is not a medical device, therapy service, emergency service, diagnostic tool, or substitute for professional care. If you are experiencing a crisis, severe anxiety, a medical emergency, or thoughts of harming yourself or others, contact emergency services or a qualified crisis support service immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
