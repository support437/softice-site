Pet medication reminder FAQ

Common questions from pet parents

Can PetCare Log remind me when my pet needs medicine?

Yes. You can organize medication names, doses, times and recurring reminder schedules for each pet.

Can I use the app for more than one pet?

The free version includes one pet profile. PetCare Log Pro unlocks unlimited pet profiles and unlimited reminders.

Can I use PetCare Log for pets other than dogs and cats?

Yes. The app can be used for rabbits, birds and other pets that need organized medication and daily-care routines.

Is PetCare Log free?

PetCare Log is free to start. Optional Pro plans unlock unlimited pets, reminders, logs and health records.

Does PetCare Log replace veterinary advice?

No. PetCare Log is an organization tool. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment or replace a licensed veterinarian.
