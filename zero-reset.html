Zero Reset questions

What is Zero Reset?

Zero Reset is a no-excuses discipline streak tracker app for daily check-ins, habit streaks, accountability, reminders, reset-to-zero motivation, quotes, and progress tracking.

What happens if I miss a day?

The core rule is simple: miss a day and the streak goes back to zero. That consequence gives the streak emotional weight and helps you protect it.

Is Zero Reset a habit tracker?

Yes, but with a stronger discipline-first style. Zero Reset is a habit streak tracker and accountability app built for people who want a clear daily rule, not a soft checklist.

Is Zero Reset good for building discipline?

Yes. Zero Reset is designed for people who want a simple daily discipline system. The streak counter, daily check-in, reminders, and reset-to-zero rule help make consistency feel visible and important.
