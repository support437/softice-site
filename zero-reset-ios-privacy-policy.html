Zero Reset for iPhone and iPad

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026
Last updated: July 29, 2026

Privacy summary: Zero Reset stores your disciplines, check-ins, progress, and preferences primarily on your device. We do not sell your personal information. We do not use advertising trackers. We do not send discipline names, descriptions, notes, or reset reasons through retention analytics.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Softice Innovations Labs LLC (“Softice,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Zero Reset application for iPhone and iPad (“Zero Reset” or the “App”).

By using Zero Reset, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information stored on your device

Zero Reset is designed primarily as an on-device discipline and consistency tracker. Information you enter into the App may include:

  • Discipline names and descriptions
  • Daily success or reset check-ins
  • Reset reasons or optional notes
  • Streaks, milestones, achievements, and progress history
  • Reminder preferences and selected reminder times
  • Appearance and other App preferences

This information is stored locally using Apple platform storage technologies, including SwiftData and system preferences.

Softice does not receive or remotely access this user-entered content unless a future feature clearly explains the transfer and obtains any consent required by law.

3. Information we may collect

When production analytics are enabled, Zero Reset may collect limited technical and usage information needed to measure app reliability, onboarding, feature adoption, and retention.

Information category Examples Purpose Linked to identity?
App activity Onboarding started or completed, first meaningful action, daily core action, reminder enabled or disabled Understand whether important app experiences are working and where users encounter friction No, unless technical circumstances require otherwise
Retention signals First open, Day 1 return, Day 7 return, and inactivity or churn indicators Measure retention and guide product improvements No
Technical information App version, build number, operating-system version, device category, event time, and export status Diagnose compatibility, reliability, and analytics delivery No
Random installation identifier An app-generated identifier that distinguishes one installation from another Prevent duplicate retention events and calculate anonymous usage patterns Not intended to identify you personally

Information excluded from analytics

Zero Reset is designed not to send the following user-entered content through retention analytics:

  • Discipline names
  • Discipline descriptions
  • Personal notes
  • Reset or failure reasons
  • Journal-style content
  • Contacts, photos, precise location, or health information

4. How information is collected

Information may be collected through:

  • Information you enter directly into the App and that remains stored locally
  • App-generated events created when you complete selected actions
  • Technical details supplied by iOS, such as the App version or operating-system version
  • Local notification authorization and reminder-state changes

Zero Reset does not require you to create an account for the current iOS version.

5. How we use information

Limited technical and analytics information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the App
  • Understand onboarding completion
  • Measure first-session success and retention
  • Improve reminders, progress displays, and recovery experiences
  • Detect crashes, errors, misuse, or technical problems
  • Evaluate product performance across supported iOS devices
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms

We do not use user-entered discipline content for advertising, profiling, or sale.

6. Local notifications

Zero Reset may ask for permission to schedule local reminders. Notification permission is optional and is controlled by Apple’s iOS notification settings.

Reminder requests are scheduled on your device. You may disable reminders within the App or through:

Settings → Notifications → Zero Reset

Denying notification permission does not prevent you from using the App’s primary discipline-tracking functions.

7. Analytics providers and service providers

Zero Reset may use a production analytics or hosting provider to receive limited events described in this policy. Any provider used will process information on our behalf for app operation, reliability, analytics, or security purposes.

We will update this policy if the production version begins using a provider whose identity or data practices require additional disclosure.

We do not authorize service providers to sell Zero Reset user data or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

8. Advertising and tracking

The current iOS version of Zero Reset does not include third-party advertising and is not designed to track users across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Zero Reset does not request App Tracking Transparency permission unless a future feature introduces activity that requires it.

9. Purchases and payment information

Zero Reset iOS version 1.0 is planned as a free-only release and does not currently offer an active StoreKit purchase or subscription flow.

If paid features are introduced later, payments will be processed by Apple through the App Store. Softice will not directly receive your complete payment-card information. This policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Limited information may be disclosed:

  • To service providers working on our behalf for hosting, analytics, reliability, or security
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid government request
  • To protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Softice, our users, or others
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable legal protections

11. Data retention

On-device information

Locally stored disciplines, check-ins, history, and preferences generally remain on your device until you delete them within the App, uninstall the App, erase the device, or otherwise remove the data through Apple platform controls.

Analytics information

Analytics information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to measure product performance, investigate reliability issues, satisfy legal obligations, and support legitimate business purposes.

Specific retention periods may depend on the production analytics provider and will be documented when that provider is activated.

12. Deleting information and privacy choices

You may manage local information by:

  • Editing or archiving disciplines within Zero Reset
  • Deleting available app records through provided controls
  • Disabling reminders
  • Changing notification permission through iOS Settings
  • Uninstalling the App to remove locally stored application data

To ask a privacy question or request deletion of information that may have reached a Softice-operated service, contact us at the email address listed below.

Because the App currently does not require an account, we may need limited technical information to determine whether we can identify the relevant analytics records. Some anonymous or aggregated data may not be reasonably attributable to a particular person.

13. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no storage system, device, network, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Children’s privacy

Zero Reset is a general productivity and self-management tool and is not directed to children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so that we can review and address the matter.

15. International users

Softice Innovations Labs LLC is based in the United States. Information processed by an activated analytics or infrastructure provider may be handled in the United States or other countries where that provider operates.

Those jurisdictions may have data-protection laws that differ from the laws in your location. We will apply protections required by applicable law.

16. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or information about data practices.

To submit a request, contact us using the information below. We may need to verify the request before responding. These rights may be subject to legal limitations and may not apply to information that cannot reasonably be linked to you.
