About Zero Reset

Zero Reset is a personal discipline and consistency tracker developed by Softice Innovations Labs LLC. It allows users to create disciplines, record daily outcomes, view streaks and progress, review activity history, and optionally schedule local reminders.

Local app data

Discipline information, check-in history, preferences, progress, and reminder settings are primarily stored locally on the user’s device using Apple platform storage technologies.

Notifications

Zero Reset may request permission to schedule local notifications. Notification permission is optional and can be changed at any time through iOS Settings. Disabling notifications does not prevent use of the app’s core tracking features.

Analytics

Zero Reset may use privacy-conscious analytics to understand onboarding completion, feature usage, app reliability, and retention. The app is designed not to transmit discipline names, notes, descriptions, or other user-entered journal content as analytics data.

No professional advice

Zero Reset is a self-management and productivity tool. It does not provide medical, psychological, legal, financial, or other professional advice.
