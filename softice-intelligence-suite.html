Google Ads API

Read-only reporting for Softice-owned campaigns.

Softice Intelligence Suite uses the Google Ads API to retrieve performance information from Google Ads accounts owned and operated by Softice Innovations Labs LLC.

✓ Used only for internal reporting and business intelligence.
✓ Restricted to authorized internal Softice personnel.
✓ No third-party advertising accounts or clients are managed.
✓ No autonomous campaign modifications are performed.
Reporting capability

Campaign and account data retrieved

  • Account descriptive name
  • Account currency and time zone
  • Campaign ID and campaign name
  • Campaign status and primary status
  • Advertising channel type
  • Campaign start and end dates
  • Campaign budget information
  • Advertising spend
  • Impressions and clicks
  • Conversions and all conversions
  • Conversion value
  • Click-through rate
  • Average cost per click
  • Cost per conversion
Internal calculations

Business metrics and summaries

Retrieved data may be organized into internal summaries such as total campaign spend, active campaign count, active daily budget, CTR, CPC, CPA, conversion totals, campaign comparisons, and budget-limitation indicators.

Supported campaigns

App campaign reporting

The current primary use is reporting on Google Ads app campaigns promoting applications developed and published by Softice Innovations Labs LLC.

Security and control

Designed around protected credentials and human decision-making.

The Suite limits its current Google Ads integration to reporting and internal analysis.

What the integration does

  • Authenticates through Google OAuth 2.0.
  • Uses a Google Ads developer token for API requests.
  • Reads authorized account and campaign performance data.
  • Converts verified results into internal dashboards and reports.
  • Labels live API data separately from demo, imported, seed, and calculated data.
  • Masks customer identifiers in diagnostic responses.
  • Provides advisory analysis for an authorized human reviewer.

What the integration does not do

  • Create or delete Google Ads accounts.
  • Create or delete campaigns.
  • Change campaign budgets.
  • Change bids, targeting, or creative assets.
  • Pause or enable campaigns automatically.
  • Upload offline or app conversions.
  • Create remarketing audiences.
  • Serve external advertising clients.
  • Allow AI recommendations to execute campaign changes.
Human oversight

All advertising decisions remain under direct human control.

Softice Intelligence Suite is a decision-support and reporting system. It may organize data, calculate performance summaries, and provide AI-assisted recommendations. It does not have authority to make autonomous changes to Google Ads campaigns. An authorized representative of Softice Innovations Labs LLC reviews the evidence and makes every final advertising and business decision.

Data handling

Google Ads data is used only for internal company operations.

Purpose limitation

Google Ads information is used for internal campaign reporting, portfolio comparisons, advertising-efficiency analysis, and business planning for Softice-owned applications.

Credential protection

OAuth credentials, refresh tokens, developer tokens, and related API secrets are maintained as encrypted backend secrets. They are not embedded in public website code or distributed inside the Android application.

No external client access

Google Ads data retrieved by the Suite is not sold, publicly displayed, or used to operate advertising accounts belonging to external customers.

Internal accountability

Important metrics are accompanied by their data source and reporting period. Assumptions and missing information are identified rather than presented as verified facts.
