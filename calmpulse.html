CalmPulse FAQ

What is CalmPulse?

CalmPulse is a wellness app for adults with anxiety relief tools, guided breathing, panic reset support, calm timer sessions, Smart Calm check-ins, and progress tracking.

Is CalmPulse free?

CalmPulse is free to start. CalmPulse Pro is optional and unlocks more calming categories, premium ambient sounds, and expanded Smart Calm support.

Can CalmPulse help with panic or stress?

CalmPulse includes quick self-care tools such as Panic Reset, 2-Minute Breathing, and Calm Timer to support stressful moments and help you pause, breathe, and reset.

Is CalmPulse medical treatment?

No. CalmPulse is a self-care wellness app and does not provide medical advice, therapy, diagnosis, or emergency support.
